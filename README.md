## 🔭 Areas of Interest & Philosophy

> *"Practical systems require simple approaches that work."*

* **Antenna Array Processing:** Linear, circular, planar, and hybrid geometries.
* **Optimization:** I almost always default to **Least-Squares**.
* **Adaptive Filtering:** I stick to **LMS-based** filters for interference/clutter cancellation.
    * *Lesson Learned:* The myth of "fast adaptive filters" I studied during my PhD shattered in the real world. Practicality beats complexity.
* **Radar Paradigms:**
    * Fascinated by **Distributed Sensing** and **Active Multistatic Radars**.
    * **Radar Imaging:** A firm believer in the Back-projection algorithm.
    * **Thinking regarding GPU:** Reconsidering sub-band approaches I used back in 2001.
